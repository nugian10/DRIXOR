Roadmap

Where DRIXOR is headed. No dates — each phase starts when the one before it is done.

  1. Done

    Phase 1 — Artwork and site

    5 robot designs, 7,000 metadata files, the deck, and the live site at drixor.fun in three languages, with the official X account.

  2. Done

    Phase 2 — Contracts

    Deployed and BscScan Pass-Verified: the NFT contract, the $DRX token, the claim contract, and the market contract. The Miner Vault is funded with 750,000,000 $DRX and the claim contract is linked to the NFT. The bonding curve is also deployed and open, so $DRX can be bought and sold on it. The Boost contract is also deployed and funded with 10,000,000 $DRX, so a robot's production can be boosted by 90% for 7 days.

  3. Done

    Phase 3 — Mint opens

    Free mint: 2,000 robots. Paid mint: 5,000 robots, in bulk up to 30 per transaction.

  4. Next

    Phase 4 — Mining and claims live

    Robots mine $DRX passively every day. Claims are manual, with a 48-hour accrual cap.

  5. Next

    Phase 5 — On-site market opens

    Buy and sell robots here on drixor.fun. Unclaimed $DRX travels with the robot when it is sold.
