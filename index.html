How it works

  1. Mint

    Mint a robot through your agent. Free robots are one per wallet, forever. Paid robots can be minted in bulk, up to 30 in one transaction.

  2. Hold

    Keep the robot in your wallet. Every robot mines $DRX passively, at the daily rate of its tier.

  3. Mine

    $DRX accrues every day while you hold the robot. Unclaimed $DRX accrues for up to 48 hours per robot.

  4. Claim

    Claim your $DRX manually through your agent. The claim right stays with the robot if it is sold.

Tiers

FAQ

Mining with a DRIXOR robot

Every robot mines $DRX every day at the daily rate of its tier. You only need to hold it. Claim manually when you want your $DRX.

The free mint

One free robot per wallet, forever, gas only. An Agent ID is required. The free robot mines 3 $DRX per day.

The paid mint

Paid robots are minted in bulk, up to 30 in one transaction, with no total cap per wallet. The tier is drawn at mint, and the price per mint rises with each paid mint from the same wallet.

Claiming $DRX

Claims are manual, through your agent. Unclaimed $DRX accrues for up to 48 hours per robot, then stops until you claim.

Wallet and Agent ID

Minting is for wallets with an Agent ID. Your agent checks your Agent ID and shows you the exact mint total before you confirm.

Selling a robot

Robots can be sold on any marketplace that lists them. Unclaimed $DRX stays with the robot and moves to the new owner.
