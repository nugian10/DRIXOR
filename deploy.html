Deploy the Contracts

Deploy the DRIXOR contracts to BNB Chain from your own browser wallet. No private key, no seed phrase — ever. Your wallet signs each transaction after you click. Steps 4–9 deploy the four contracts, fund the Miner Vault and link the Claim contract. Steps 10–13 deploy and open the $DRX bonding curve. Steps 14–15 deploy and fund the $DRX boost.

Safety first. This page will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Only connect your browser wallet. If any page or person asks for your seed phrase, it is a scam — stop immediately. Deployment costs real BNB. Your wallet shows the exact cost before you confirm each transaction — check it before you sign.
