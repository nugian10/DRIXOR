How the market will work

List a robot

Owners list a robot at a price in BNB, through their agent.

Buy a robot

Buyers buy through their agent. The robot moves to the buyer, and any unclaimed $DRX moves with it.

Cancel a listing

A listing can be cancelled by its owner any time before it sells.

Fees

A marketplace fee and a creator royalty will apply. The rates will be announced together with the market contract — no numbers are set yet.
